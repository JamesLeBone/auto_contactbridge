# Privacy Policy

All data is owned by you, who use the app.

## Data Storage

Data is stored on your device.  If you choose to authenticate with Meta to lookup contacts for
messages, that communication is between you and Meta.  A session reference is stored on your device
until you are logged out.

### What is stored

* Contact list
* Login session with Meta

## Data Removal

In a future version, this app will allow you to clear your contacts list and logout of Meta.
